# What is ELN?

ELN is a digital tool that stands for *electronic lab notebook*. As digitalisation in scientific research is rapidly developing, nowadays most of the ELNs are not just merely tools are jotting down notes for what was done in the lab, but also are integrated with tools for analyses. 

The significance in the application of ELNs is tightly related to the [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) for scientific data management: **F**indability, **A**ccessibility, **I**nteroperability and **R**euse of digital assets. In short, we aspire to strengthen the network of data management and sharing among all fellow scientists.

# What is Kladde?
Kladde is a ELN based on the open-sourced [^1] [cheminfo](https://cheminfo.github.io/) that is specifically customized for students and researchers at the Friedrich-Schiller-University-Jena. The major features of Kladde include, but not limited to, are a big variety of spectra simulation tools (e.g. IR, NMR, MS), functions integrated with [NMRium](https://app.nmrium.com/), chemoinformatics and so on. 



There are currently a lot of choices of open-sourced ELNs available among academic institutes, with which we are in close connection, such as [Chemotion](https://chemotion.net/) and [eLabFTW](https://www.elabftw.net/). 

[^1]: open-source means the source code of the programmes are published e.g. on GitHub Pages and are open for viewing and revisions.
