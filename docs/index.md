# A warm welcome from us and thank you for using Kladde! 
Here you can find tutorials and help for the big variety of functions available in this *electronic lab notebook (ELN)*.

## A tutorial page especially designated for **Bachelor students of Chemistry in their first synthetic-chemistry lab practical.**

For the suggested **complete** workflow please check out 

- [How to start](new-entry.md): start your entry and prepare for the synthesis
    - [Adding chemical equation](adding-chemical-equation.md): quick look at the descriptions of the buttons in the chemical editor
    - [Snippets](snippets.md): use of shortcuts in your descriptions
- Sample Analysis: entry for information such as melting point, GC-result and IR-spectra
- Analyses: learn to use different available tools ([IR](ir.md), [NMR](nmr.md)) to anaylse your products
